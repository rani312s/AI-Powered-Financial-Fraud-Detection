About NeuroFinance GPT

"NeuroFinance GPT: Explainable Deep Learning for Financial Fraud Prediction Using Multi-Modal Data" is an advanced academic prototype designed to address the black-box challenge of traditional AI fraud detection models.

Key Architectural Components

  • Multi-Modal Feature Fusion: Integrates numerical, categorical, temporal, and behavioral indicators.
  • ML Risk Classifier: Predicts continuous fraud probability (0–100%) mapped to Low, Medium, and High risk tiers.
  • Explainable AI (SHAP): Calculates feature impact attributions for full model transparency.
  • GPT Natural Language Summary: Converts technical feature weights into plain-English explanations.
  • Anti-False-Positive Nuance: High monetary amounts alone never trigger fraud alerts without contextual anomalies.

System Benefits

  • Reduces unnecessary false fraud alerts for high-value legitimate transfers (e.g. tuition fees).
  • Empowers non-technical fraud analysts with human-understandable AI reasoning.
  • Maintains an audit trail of all analyzed transactions in a local SQLite database.
  • Protects financial privacy with client-side environment variable security.
